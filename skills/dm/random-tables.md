# Random Event & Encounter Tables

Roll d20 on the relevant table when entering a new scene or after a rest. On 1-10, nothing notable happens; describe the neon, the rain and the noise. On 11-20, use the corresponding entry. The DM expands each seed to fit the current situation. Pick enemies from `bestiary.md` and validate them.

**Corporate War modifier:** at War stage 5+ (see `cyberpunk.md`), add +2 to every roll on these tables. Results over 20 count as 20.

---

## Street (city districts, markets, megabuildings)

| d20 | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 11 | A street fight spills out of a bar | DC 12 Perception to spot a pickpocket lifting credchips in the crowd |
| 12 | A vendor sells chrome "off a truck" at half price | Black-market cyberware: 1 in 20 has a backdoor or glitch |
| 13 | Newsfeeds and billboards break a story tied to an active quest | Tie to current quest hooks; corp spin vs. street rumor |
| 14 | An NPC the player helped (or burned) recognizes them | Use an existing NPC or create a minor one |
| 15 | NCPD stop-and-search | DC 13 Persuasion or Deception, or a bribe; failure +1 Heat |
| 16 | A kid offers to sell a data shard they "found" | Cheap intel, possibly a trap or a corp's lost property |
| 17 | Fixer pings the player's holo with a gig | Side quest seed; pay scaled to level |
| 18 | A gang or corp agent approaches with a proposition | Tie to the faction with highest or lowest reputation |
| 19 | Something goes wrong: a cyberpsycho alert, a building fire, a car crash | Skill challenge, DC 14-16; MaxTac may arrive |
| 20 | Every screen in the block glitches with the same message | Rogue AI foreshadowing, tied to the main threat |

## Highway / Travel (expressways, metro, AV flights)

| d20 | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 11 | A nomad convoy passing the other way shares news over comms | Info about the destination or the war front |
| 12 | Wreck blocking the road; survivors need help | DC 12 Athletics or Cybertech to clear or assist |
| 13 | Drone shadowing the vehicle | DC 13 Perception; someone is tracking the player |
| 14 | Corporate checkpoint | Papers, bribe, or Driving DC 14 to run it (+1 Heat on a run) |
| 15 | Gang toll roadblock | Social or combat encounter, CR appropriate to level |
| 16 | Toxic storm, smog surge or acid rain | Travel delay; DC 12 CON save without a sealed vehicle or 1 level of exhaustion |
| 17 | Abandoned vehicle with signs of a hasty exit | DC 14 Investigation reveals who took them and why |
| 18 | A wounded courier crawls out of a ditch | DC 12 Medicine to stabilize; they're carrying something hot |
| 19 | A shortcut through a war-torn or gang-held district | Risk/reward choice for the player |
| 20 | AV dogfight or corporate strike overhead | Debris hazard, DC 13 DEX save or 2d6 bludgeoning |

|-----|-------|-------|

| 14 | Landmines or a raider snare | DC 13 Survival to spot before triggering |
| 15 | Natural hazard: sinkhole, flash flood, radiation pocket | DEX or CON save DC 13 or take 1d6 damage |
| 16 | Raiders or scavengers attack | Combat, CR appropriate to level; use terrain and surprise |
| 17 | A hidden nomad cache | DC 14 Investigation to find; fuel, ammo, MaxDocs |
| 18 | Militech or Arasaka convoy moving war materiel | Tie to the Corporate War clock |
| 19 | A stranded traveler or runaway corpo | Potential ally, informant or bounty |
| 20 | A dead zone where every device fails, and something moves in it | Rogue AI incursion: rogue-drone or rogue-android plus a hazard |

## Facility (corporate towers, labs, gang hideouts, server farms; replaces Dungeon)

| d20 | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 11 | Guard chatter echoing from a vent | Perception DC 13 to learn patrol timing |
| 12 | A dead edgerunner with a cracked deck and a last log | Lore drop or a warning about what's ahead |
| 13 | Security trap: laser grid, pressure plate, gas vent | DC 14 Investigation or Netrunning to spot; DEX save DC 13 to avoid |
| 14 | Lockdown: shutters drop, lights go red | Creates urgency; Breach to reopen |
| 15 | A lone guard or drone off its patrol route | Combat, CR appropriate; can be taken down quietly |
| 16 | Signs of another crew already inside | Someone else is running this job |
| 17 | A locked terminal guarding a minor prize | Breach Protocol vs. the site's ICE |
| 18 | Structural damage from the war: collapsing floor, exposed wiring | DEX save DC 14 or 2d6 damage |
| 19 | Maintenance shaft or hidden service elevator | Perception or Investigation DC 15 |
| 20 | The boss's floor: heavy security, a mech in the hangar, Black ICE humming | Foreshadowing; the player can engage or avoid |

## The Net (used while breaching or diving)

| d20 | Event | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 11 | A Netwatch scanner sweeps past | DC 13 Netrunning check to avoid notice |
| 12 | Leftover data from a previous runner | Clue tied to an active quest |
| 13 | ICE spikes a security tier unexpectedly | Raise the site's ICE by one tier for this breach |
| 14 | Another netrunner is in the same subnet | Rival, ally or bait; social or Netrunning contest |
| 15 | A daemon is already planted here | Someone set you up; enemies get +2 to saves this fight |
| 16 | Corrupted camera feed shows something it shouldn't | Clue or misdirection |
| 17 | Hidden admin back door | Next breach on this subnet has advantage |
| 18 | Corp counter-trace starts | Start a 4-segment "Traced" clock; full = a kill team arrives |
| 19 | A voice that isn't human speaks to the player | Rogue AI contact; it wants something |
| 20 | Glimpse beyond the Blackwall | DC 15 WIS save or lose 1d4 Humanity; gain a major lore clue |
